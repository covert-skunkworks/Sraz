# How to play Sraz

Sraz is a quiz board game for two to four players. Every move is paid for with a correct answer. Capture ground, defeat
enemy pegs and be the last army on the board.

## Goal

Eliminate every other army. The last player who still has pegs on the board wins.

## Setting up

* **Players:** 2, 3 or 4 (default 2). Enter a name for each.
* **Board:** 8 x 8 (default) or 10 x 10 tiles.
* **Answer time:** 9, 15, 30, 60 or 600 seconds per question (default 15).
* **Question language:** English or Croatian.

Each player has **five pegs** in the colour of their army, set up around their **flag tile** in a corner of the board. With
two players the flags are in diagonally opposite corners.

## The board

* Every tile carries a **category** that decides which kind of question you get: Sport, History, Geography, Nature, Music,
  General, Art, Science, or Random (a question from any category).
* **Black tiles** are holes. Nothing can enter them.
* **Flag tiles** sit in the corners. You cannot step on your own flag tile, but you can capture an enemy one.

## Pegs and ranks

Every peg earns points, from 0 to 14, and its rank comes from them:

| Points | Rank | Tries per turn |
|---|---|---|
| 0 - 4 | Kmet (serf) | 1 |
| 5 - 9 | Vojnik (soldier) | 2 |
| 10 - 14 | Vitez (knight) | 3 |

The number of tries is how many times that peg may act in one turn. A serf gets one move, a knight up to three.

## Your turn

1. Pick one of your pegs, then pick one of the four neighbouring tiles (up, down, left, right). You may move onto an empty
   tile, an enemy peg or an enemy flag tile. You may not move onto a hole, your own flag tile or one of your own pegs.
2. You are asked a question. Choose one of four answers before the time runs out. If time runs out, the answer that is
   highlighted at that moment counts.
3. The result decides what happens (see below). A wrong answer costs one try but nothing else.

Your turn ends when:

* no peg of yours has a try left or a free neighbouring tile,
* a battle has been fought (won or lost),
* you have tried to capture an enemy flag tile (won or lost), or
* you pass.

## Moving to an empty tile

Answer one question. If it is **correct**, the peg moves there and gains **1 point**. If it is **wrong**, the peg stays where
it is and uses up a try.

You gain points only while you still hold a flag tile of your own colour that no peg is standing on. If you have lost all
your flags, your pegs stop earning points.

## Capturing a flag tile

Answer one question. If it is **correct**, the tile changes to your colour and your peg moves onto it (+1 point). Either
way, your turn ends.

## Battles

Moving onto an enemy peg starts a battle with **two questions**:

* First question **wrong**: nothing happens. Your turn ends.
* First question **right**, second **wrong**: your peg loses 1 point. Your turn ends.
* **Both right:** the defender loses **5 points**.
  * If the defender drops below 0 points, it is removed from the board.
  * Otherwise it is pushed back one tile in the direction of your attack. Pegs standing behind it are pushed along too. A
    peg pushed into a hole or off the edge of the board is lost.
  * Your peg moves into the freed tile and you gain points for both questions (up to 14 in total).

Your turn ends after the battle.

## Questions

* Four answers are shown, in random order. Only one is right.
* The question comes from the category of the tile you are moving to. Random tiles and flag tiles use any category.
* A question is not repeated in a category until all of its questions have been used once.

## Winning

When all pegs of every other player are gone, the last army wins. An eliminated player's turn is skipped. If you cannot or do
not want to move, you can pass.

## Playing

Play on one computer, taking turns, or over a local network (LAN), where one player hosts the game and the others join. The
window can be resized freely.

Controls: arrow keys and Enter, or the mouse. Space passes your turn, Esc leaves the game, and the sound can be switched off
and on.
